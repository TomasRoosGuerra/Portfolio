TOMASROOS Chord Finder

Drop a song.
Get the chords.

Any audio file on your device works: MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, or a voice memo of you playing. You get a beat-synced chord chart, guitar shapes and a capo suggestion.

The song never leaves your device: all listening happens in this browser tab. Streaming links (Spotify, YouTube) can't be read; use a file you own.
